This module allows respondents to modify and update their answers to a
survey after a first submission.

When enabled on a survey, respondents who complete or revisit a
completed survey have the option to edit their existing responses rather
than being locked in read-only mode or forced to restart a blank
submission.

Use Cases / Context
===================

In standard Odoo, once a respondent submits a survey
(``survey.user_input`` reaches state ``done``), the access link
transitions into read-only mode. The respondent can only consult their
answers or start a completely new submission from scratch without
retrieving their previous answers.

However, several business scenarios require respondents to update their
answers without starting over:

- Registration and enrollment forms (e.g., updating contact details,
  arrival dates, or uploaded documents before a deadline).
- Long multi-section questionnaires where a user realizes they made a
  mistake or omitted an item right after submitting.
- Periodic self-evaluations or profile forms where participants adjust
  their responses over time.

Configuration
=============

To allow respondents to edit their answers:

1. Go to **Surveys > Surveys** and select or create a survey.
2. Open the **Options** tab.
3. In the **Participants** section, check the **Allow editing answers**
   option (``allow_editing``).
4. Save the survey.

Usage
=====

**Architecture & Token Lifecycle**

Odoo surveys rely on a dual-token mechanism:

1. **Survey Access Token (``survey.access_token``)**: Unique hash
   identifying the survey definition (e.g.,
   ``/survey/start/<survey_token>``).
2. **Answer Access Token (``survey.user_input.access_token``)**: Unique
   hash identifying a specific participant's submission session (e.g.,
   ``/survey/<survey_token>/<answer_token>``).

When a respondent initiates a survey via
``/survey/start/<survey_token>``, Odoo sets an HTTP cookie in the
browser:

.. code:: text

   survey_<survey_token>=<answer_token>

with a default validity of 24 hours. Subsequent visits to
``/survey/<survey_token>`` read this cookie to match the current
respondent session.

**Navigation and Editing Workflow**

1. **Initial Submission**:

   - The respondent completes the questions and clicks **Submit**.
   - The submission is marked as done (``state = 'done'``), and the
     completion ``end_datetime`` is recorded.
   - The respondent lands on the native completion screen.

2. **Consultation and Edit Option**:

   - On the completion screen, the respondent can:

     - Click **Review your answers**
       (``/survey/print/<survey_token>?answer_token=<answer_token>&review=True``)
       to view their submitted responses in read-only mode.
     - Click **Edit your answers**
       (``/survey/start/<survey_token>?answer_token=<answer_token>&edit=1``)
       to enter modification mode.

   - Direct visits or page refreshes on
     ``/survey/<survey_token>/<answer_token>`` remain on the completion
     screen; the survey is not prematurely restarted unless the user
     explicitly clicks the edit button.

3. **Editing Mode**:

   - When **Edit your answers** is clicked, the system executes:

     - The response transitions from ``done`` to ``in_progress`` (logged
       in chatter).
     - The pagination pointer is reset, placing the respondent at the
       first question.
     - All previous responses remain pre-filled in the form.
     - Backward navigation is enabled across all pages.

4. **Resubmission**:

   - The respondent edits their answers and clicks **Submit**.
   - Answer lines are updated in-place, ensuring no duplicate line
     entries are created.
   - The submission returns to ``done``, updating ``end_datetime`` and
     logging the completion state change in chatter.

**Respondent Lifecycles: Public vs Logged-In Users**

- **Registered / Logged-in Users**:

  - The submission is permanently associated with the user's partner
    record (``partner_id``).
  - Even if the browser cookie is deleted or the user accesses the
    survey from a different device, visiting
    ``/survey/start/<survey_token>`` automatically retrieves their
    existing submission.
  - If the survey was previously completed, the user is presented with
    the completion screen with the options to review or edit.

- **Public / Anonymous Users**:

  - The submission is not tied to a registered partner account.
  - Identification relies on:

    1. The active browser cookie (``survey_<survey_token>``), or
    2. The tokenized URL (``/survey/<survey_token>/<answer_token>``),
       such as those sent via email invitation links.

  - As long as the cookie remains active in their browser, a public user
    can complete the survey, consult their answers, and click **Edit
    your answers** to modify their submission.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

**Public User Session Retention**

- **Anonymous submissions without a tokenized URL**: If a public user
  completes an open public survey directly via the generic link
  ``/survey/start/<survey_token>``, their session identity is stored
  only in the browser cookie ``survey_<survey_token>``.

  - If the user clears their browser cache/cookies, switches browsers,
    or opens the generic survey link on another device, Odoo cannot
    technically identify them (to prevent anonymous users from accessing
    other respondents' data). In this situation, visiting the generic
    link starts a new blank survey.
  - However, if the survey was distributed via email invitations (which
    include the unique ``answer_token`` in the URL), public users can
    return and edit their answers from any device at any time using that
    link.
  - A possible roadmap improvement could be an option to email a
    resumption link with the answer token upon first submission for
    anonymous respondents.

**Scoring and Certification**

- For surveys with scoring and certification, retaking or editing
  answers currently retains the standard certification behavior (e.g.,
  if certification is already issued).
